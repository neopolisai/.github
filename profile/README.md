## Your context, put to work.

We're **Neopolis**, the company behind **Relay** and **Navii**. We build tools that help people put the context behind their work and experience to use, with their approval.

**[Neopolis](https://neopolis.ai/)** · **[Relay](https://relayto.ai/)** · **[Navii](https://heynavii.ai/)**

### Relay

**Don't just send a doc. Send a relay.**

Share a proposal, deck or handoff with the background and purpose behind it. The person reads the work. Their agent gets the context you approved, and questions that need you come back to you.

```text
relay {what} to {who} for {why}
```

**[Try a relay](https://relayto.ai/try/annual-proposal)** · **[Connect your agent](https://relayto.ai/mcp)** · **[Explore the public repository](https://github.com/neopolisai/relaytoai)**

### Open format. Clear boundaries.

The [Relay repository](https://github.com/neopolisai/relaytoai) contains the context-package JSON Schema, a worked example, the agent skill, the extraction standard and connection docs, under Apache 2.0. Build with the format or help improve it through issues and pull requests.

The hosted Relay service, app and user data are not open source.
